Learn Healthy Ways to Reduce Stress & Depression

Your Mental Health Matters ❤️

Everyone experiences stress and sadness at times. Small daily habits can improve your emotional well-being and help you feel better over time.

😌 Ways to Reduce Stress

  • Practice deep breathing for 5 minutes.
  • Take short breaks while studying or working.
  • Listen to calming music.
  • Spend time in nature.
  • Exercise for at least 30 minutes daily.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Limit social media usage.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours every night.

💙 Ways to Manage Depression

  • Talk to someone you trust.
  • Do one enjoyable activity every day.
  • Avoid isolating yourself.
  • Set small daily goals.
  • Maintain a healthy routine.
  • Eat balanced meals.
  • Seek professional help if symptoms continue.
